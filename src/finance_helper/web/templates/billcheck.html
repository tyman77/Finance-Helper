Bill Check

Reads the invoice attached to every unpaid bill in Bill.com and compares it, field by field, with what was entered — vendor, invoice number, invoice date, due date, total. Anything that disagrees lands here before the bill is paid. Nothing is changed in Bill.com: fix the entry there, mark it fixed, and the next run re-verifies it.
